How to use Expense Tracker

A quick guide to every feature

1 Get started

  • Tap Create account. Enter your name (optional), your email twice, and a password twice. The eye icon shows what you typed.
  • Check your email (and spam) for the confirmation link, tap it, then come back and sign in. Or tap Continue with Google to skip that.
  • Pick your currency (£, $ or ₦). You can change it later in Settings.

2 The five tabs

  • Home: your total balance, this month's income and spending, bills coming up this week, and where your money went. Tap + Add to record something. Pull down to refresh.
  • Transactions: every entry, newest first. Tap one to edit it, press and hold to delete it, and tap a tag at the top to filter. Import is for bank files.
  • Budgets: type a monthly amount next to a category. It carries forward each month until you change it.
  • Reports: choose This month, Last 3 months or Last 12 months to see income versus spending, your savings rate and spending by category.
  • Settings: your account, currency and everything below.

3 Add a transaction

  • Tap + Add and choose Expense or Income.
  • Type the amount and a description. The category may fill in by itself.
  • Pick the date, the account and a category. For one purchase that covers several things, tap Split into categories.
  • Optionally add tags and notes, then tap Save.

4 Import from your bank

  • Download a CSV from your bank's app or website. In Transactions, tap Import, pick the file, check the preview and tap Import.
  • If your credit card shows spending as positive numbers, switch on that option before importing.

5 Settings, one by one

  • Account: change your name. Your email is shown above it.
  • Currency: switch between £, $ and ₦. Only the symbol changes; amounts are not converted.
  • Accounts: add accounts such as Joint savings, with an opening balance. Archiving hides one without losing its history.
  • Recurring: add bills and subscriptions such as Netflix or rent. The app also suggests ones it spots.
  • Goals: create a savings goal with a target, then tap it to Add or Withdraw money.
  • Categories: add your own, or press and hold one to delete it.
  • Category rules: teach the app that, for example, tesco means Groceries, so future entries are sorted automatically.
  • Sign out, or Delete account. Deleting removes your account and all its data permanently.

6 Tips

  • Set budgets first, add transactions as you spend, and check Reports at the end of the month.
