Expense Tracker

Delete your account

Last updated 25 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Expense Tracker account and all the data in it, either from inside the app or by emailing the developer.

1 Delete it in the app (fastest)

  • Open Expense Tracker and sign in.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2 Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email icanbe444@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Expense Tracker account". We'll delete the account and reply to confirm.

3 What is deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • All financial data you entered: transactions, accounts and balances, budgets, recurring bills and income, savings goals and contributions, and imported statement data.

We do not keep a copy of this data after deletion for our own use. See the privacy policy for how data is handled while your account exists.

Questions? Email icanbe444@gmail.com.
